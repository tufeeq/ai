Before you start

Does OPERON replace our accounting or CRM system?

It provides operational records and a decision workflow. Use validated CSV imports today; live system connectors remain future integration work.

Will it automatically send messages or transfer money?

No. Current execution creates internal work. Payment recording documents money already received and does not initiate a transaction.

Is the public demo a production account?

No. It uses synthetic data and local browser storage. Production team access requires deployment of the included persistent server.

What does it cost?

Commercial subscriptions and checkout have not launched. The pilot brief helps define scope and evaluation criteria; it is not a paid order or pricing commitment.
