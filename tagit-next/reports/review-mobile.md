# Review: mobile, Arabic RTL, accessibility, resilience and performance (agent 5)

Setup: the repo was served locally (`python3 -m http.server`). Playwright and Chromium drove the page, and the quote service was mocked with `page.route`. The mock returned a scanner with 12 rows (sub-dollar prices, negative moves, long Latin names), `/api/quotes`, `/api/live` → 404 and an empty relay. The viewports were 360×740, 390×844, 768, 1280 and 1920, each in light and dark. Automated in-page scans covered horizontal overflow, bidi order (the visual position of the first and last character of every numeric token), WCAG contrast (effective background, opacity chain), focus containment, CDP heap and DOM metrics, and long tasks. The screenshots referenced below are in the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/shots/`). `before-*` means before the fix and `final-*`, `a-*`, `g-*` mean after.

Verified OK (no change needed):
- **Performance:** the 1 s re-render costs about 10 ms/s of script. Over 3 min the heap stayed at 5.9–6.1 MB, the DOM at about 4.3k nodes and listeners at 58. The only long task was one 74 ms at startup. With 4× CPU throttling (phone) it was 1.8 s script per minute, and 8 long tasks with a maximum of 66 ms.
- **Hidden tab:** 0 requests and 0 script time over 40 s. There are no duplicate timers.
- **Cold start, timeout and malformed JSON:** the page shows "جارٍ المسح…", then the cold-start notice, then an honest error. It never crashes, and malformed payloads are rejected.
- **After an outage:** rows stay, but they are marked `سعر غير حديث` with the red `يتقادم/قديم` tag and live plans drop to 0. The open sheet says "الاتصال بخدمة البيانات منقطع" (`r-after-outage-sheet.png`).
- **Keyboard:** `/` search, ↑/↓ row navigation, and Esc for the sheet all work. Tap targets are all ≥ 24 px except the brand link (20 px tall) and the Sharia `<summary>` (21 px).

## Remaining (other agents' files; one-line fixes using `f.ltr`)
- `src/views/dossier.js` `.why`: `${f.num(v,1)}×` still flips to `×2.9`. Use `f.ltr(\`${f.num(v, 1)}×\`)`.
- `src/core/risk.js` detail strings build their own `$44.8M`, `+310.2%` and `2026-06-30`, which flip inside Arabic sentences. Wrap them in isolates.
- `src/views/evidence.js`: filter labels such as `قيمة ٣ د < $50K`, and the date ranges `2026-01-02 → 2026-09-28` in `<small>` elements.
- **`evidence-release.json` hashes** need refreshing (by the lead; I was told not to edit it). `app.js`, `style.css`, `src/format.js` and `src/views/status.js` changed, and `src/backoff.js` is new.
- Nice to have: view and dossier tabs lack arrow-key roving and `aria-controls`, and the translucent top bar lets rows show through while scrolling.
